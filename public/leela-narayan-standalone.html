Biography & Philosophy

Devotion to the Divine Art of Carnatic Music

Leela Narayan is a trained Carnatic vocalist, music educator, performing artist, public speaker and entrepreneur based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. With over a decade of dedicated training in Carnatic music, she is passionate about bringing Indian classical and devotional music closer to learners of all ages and backgrounds.

As the founder of Leela Narayan School of Performing Arts (LNSOPA), Leela conducts Carnatic Vocal, Devaranama, Bhajans and Light Music classes through both online and offline formats. Her teaching philosophy goes beyond learning songs—she focuses on developing voice culture, confidence, musical discipline, stage presence and a genuine love for music.

A strong believer that music should be accessible to everyone, Leela particularly encourages women, beginners, and adults who wish to restart their musical journey.
